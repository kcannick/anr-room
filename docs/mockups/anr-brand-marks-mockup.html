The mark, the lockups, the icons

Assets 0.1, 0.2 and 0.5 from the promo inventory. Everything after them in the build order is drawn from these, so they are the ones to get right first. Every block on this page is one CSS rule sized by a single number, which is what the SVG master will be.

0.1

The A&R block markThe skewed square holding A&R. It is the part of the brand that survives at 48px, so it is drawn for 48px first.

Construction13°
A&R

A square of side S, skewed −13° on X, corner radius .04S. The three letters are Archivo 900 at .39S, tracking −.05em, counter-skewed so they stay upright. The angle never changes and the type never slants.

Clear space.5S
A&R

Nothing else inside half a side on every edge. The lockups below sit on this rule, and so does every ad. Minimum size on screen is 24px; below that use the favicon variant.

SIGN-OFF

Four decisionsOne decided, three to approve. Then the SVG masters get cut.

1
Favicon at 16 and 32 uses the ampersand alone.The three letters do not survive at 16px. The ampersand keeps the shape and the colour. From 48 up it is always A&R.
2
The article is dropped from marks and lockups. Decided.[A&R] Team and [A&R] Meeting, never The [A&R] Meeting: the article at descriptor size was larger than the A&R and unbalanced the mark. The full names, with the article, stay in copy and titles.
3
Four fills and no more: green, paper green, mono ink, purple.Gold never fills the block. Red never fills the block. A white block on dark ground is not in the set.
4
Icons are 2px square-capped strokes; the vote icon is the dial.Round caps would soften the whole set against the 13° block. The alternative for vote was a hand or a ballot, which say election, not rating.
