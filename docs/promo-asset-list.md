# Promotional design assets — The A&R Team, A&R Room, A&R Service Pack, The A&R Meeting

Working inventory of every graphic, template and print piece needed to promote the four
properties, with what already exists in the repo or the `anr-brand` skill and what still
has to be made. A&R Wars is out of scope here except where a Service Pack asset feeds it.

Status key: **EXISTS** (ship it as is) · **TEMPLATE** (a template exists, render per
instance) · **NEEDED** (nothing built).

Every asset follows the brand skill: Archivo 800/900 display, Space Mono for every number
and label, the 13° block on small formats, the big cut only where there is no type over
it. Colour by meaning: green = live / scoring / the Team and the Room; purple = the
Service Pack and anything head-to-head; gold = money and first place only; red = closing.
Plain copy, no slang, no emoji, no exclamation marks.

## Rules that decide the layout before the design starts

- **The wall:** a Meeting asset never mentions placement or the pack; a Pack asset never
  mentions scores. No graphic implies top-rated records get placed.
- **Prizes go to A&Rs only.** Gold appears on the $500, the $150 and rank 1, and nowhere else.
- **Seal:** a vote count may appear on any live graphic; the average or the A/B split never
  appears before the reveal. The Recap names panel is alphabetised for the same reason.
- **PII:** public graphics carry display name, city and points. Never email or phone.
- **Small first:** design the mark for 48px and the ads for the thumbnail. The block, not
  the cut, carries the brand on anything under 300px.
- **Terminology:** A&Rs, Session, "Series 3", "Predict the Average". Brand names unchanged.
