The sequence

  1. Record. The strip, as live today, with the handle moved under the title.
  2. Rating. Tap a number on the phone: the Rating tile pops in. Tap another: it changes.
  3. Prediction. Move the slider: the purple tile follows it live.
  4. Locked. Lock it in: the strip turns green, "Prediction locked in", a lock sound.
  5. Processing. The card rises under the camera, ticking, about a second and a half.
  6. Reveal. The average counts up, the tier lands with its own sound, then points and the round grade. It holds about eight seconds.
  7. After. The card drops away, the QR card and the call to action come back, and the strip keeps Average and Points until Next record.

Sounds (all made in the page, no files)

  1. Rating tap: one soft tick. Slider: a quiet tick per tenth.
  2. Lock: a two-note latch.
  3. Bullseye: a rising four-note fanfare. Sharp / Close: a shorter rise. Off / Way off: a low two-note drop.

What is new versus live

  1. The strip gains the two tiles and the locked bar.
  2. The reveal is one row in the QR card's slot (y 898–1078). The mandatory elements are never covered: the header, the Submit Music call to action, the primary and secondary 16:9. Nor are the fire meter, the viewer count, the ticker or the chat.
  3. The strip source polls faster while a record is up, so the slider reads live.
