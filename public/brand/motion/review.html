2.16 Starting soon loopTwenty seconds, seamless, for the OBS media source before the show. The headline slot is left empty for a countdown text source. 16:9 and 9:16.

starting-story-guide.png
What moves
The card is the key art and holds still. Under the empty zone, four lines take turns, five seconds each: Live music review · Rate the records, Predict the average · $500 to the month’s Top A&R · Submit your music, makinitmag.com/review. The 13° pager marks which one is up.
A line fades out to nothing before the next fades in, and the first and last frames are pixel-identical (checked), so the loop point never shows. The number counting down is the rest of the motion.
OBS setup
Media Source → the .mp4, Loop on, "Restart playback when source becomes active" off. Then any countdown that drives a Text source (the bundled countdown script, or the Countdown Timer plugin) laid over it.
Wide: text source at x 112, y 540, in a 1100×240 box, left-aligned, Space Mono Bold 200, colour #EAE9F2. Story: x 72, y 700, 936×260, Space Mono Bold 186. The guide stills show the fit. Space Mono is free from Google Fonts; install it on the streaming machine once.
Without a countdown
Render with &label=Starting%20soon and the zone stays empty under a "Starting soon" label, for a day the timer is not set up.
