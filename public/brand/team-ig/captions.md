## Stories (no caption)

Add Instagram's **link sticker** in the dashed box shown on the review sheet
(`guides/story-*-guides.png`).

| Story | When | Sticker target |
|---|---|---|
| `story-open.png` | every day at 6:00 PM ET, when the drop opens | anr.makinitmag.com/daily (or the day's open drop) |
| `story-join.png` | once a week | makinitmag.com/ANR |
| `story-results.png` | every day at 6:00 AM ET, when the Daily Countdown posts | anr.makinitmag.com/account#results |

`story-results` takes the day it reports on: `team-ig.html?p=story-results&day=Oct 16, 2026`
prints "Daily Drop · Oct 16, 2026" (the release date the set is named by). Without `day` it
reads "Daily Drop", which works as an evergreen template. It never shows a score, average or
rank: the Countdown post is the reveal; this story points A&Rs to their own points and grade.
Add the Countdown post to the story as well (share the post to the story) so the story leads to it.
