A&R Room key art and the weekly post

Revised 2026-09-11: the [A&R] ROOM lockup is the title, the headline sits under it in size, the weekly date is numeric (MM.DD.YY) in Space Mono, and there are no full stops. One template. With no date it is the evergreen key art (2.1): the day and the time, nothing else. With a date it is that week's post (2.3) and every platform thumbnail comes off the same file (2.4): 16:9 at 1920×1080 and 1280×720, 9:16 at 1080×1920, 4:5 at 1080×1350, 1:1 at 1080×1080. The lockup and block are the stage 1 masters. Green is the show's colour.
