Artist share graphics

The assets other people post for you. One template, two properties, and the wall between them holds: the Service Pack graphic never carries a score, and the Meeting graphic never carries a pack call to action. Stage 4 rules carry over: the lockup is the title, the artist's name sits under it in size, dates are numeric in Space Mono, no full stops. "Artist Name" and "Song Title" are placeholders; every field is a parameter.
