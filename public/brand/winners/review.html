Winner posts

One portrait graphic per person, posted as an Instagram collab so it lands on their feed too. Four posts from one template: the daily #1s from the recap, and the weekly winners with what they have earned. Every field on them is something the app already knows at the 3PM publish: rank, score and drop date for a record; grade, points, bullseyes, city, title and photo for an A&R. Single graphics, not carousels: the person is the whole card, and the link rides on the field.

Top Track · Top A&R of the DayEvery day's #1 record and #1 A&R, each celebrated on their own post. Five things on the card and no more: the lockup, the stacked title with its date, the person, one line of numbers, the field.

Top A&R of the Day
TOP A&R / of the Day · 09.12.26 / A&R Name / Producer · Atlanta, GA / photo / [A] 412 points · 3 bullseyes / Join the A&R Team · makinitmag.com/ANR
The letter grade stays in the block device, the same skewed green block that holds A&R; points and bullseyes follow it as words on one line instead of three labelled cells. Title is the profile's primary category; from is the profile's city. The photo is the profile photo when there is one; without it the name runs full width (third card).

Straps

Written from your two announcement lines. If you want them word for word as you typed them ("Announcing Placement in Next $1,000 Tournament"), say so and they swap in.
The week
Built: the console's "Winners of the week" block picks a week (Monday to Sunday, defaulting to the last completed one) and renders both cards live with captions.
The rule as built is a default: highest room average across the week's published drops for the track, most points across them for the A&R. Say if it should be something else.

Top Track of the Week is the record with the highest room average across the week's published drops (ties: more votes, then the earlier day). Top A&R of the Week is the most points summed across those drops. Grade and bullseyes are computed over that A&R's votes in the week.
