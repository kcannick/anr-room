The four brand ads

One audience, one ask, one button each, at feed (1080×1350) and story (1080×1920). One template drives all eight, and the block and lockups are the stage 1 masters, not redrawn. The 13° cut carries the field with the call to action; no line of type touches it. Copy is plain and direct. Read the copy column as the thing to approve.
