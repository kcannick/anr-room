Notes for review
  1. One tab, "My results", takes the place of the two greyed tabs (Sessions, Rounds). It sits second; Earn points stays the default. The results email links straight to this tab.
  2. Points only, no rank. Rank is revealed in the weekly announcement, so it is not shown here for a day or a week.
  3. Today's drop is first. The point of the visit is that they rate today's records, so that card sits above their history.
  4. The week is Monday to Sunday, by the day a drop opened. The arrows page back one week at a time.
  5. A closed day with no results yet shows no numbers: only how many records they rated and when results come out.
  6. A day they missed is still listed, so the gap is visible. It does not open: results are for the days they took part in.
  7. A live session appears in the same list with a tag.
