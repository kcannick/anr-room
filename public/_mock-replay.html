Replay the Day · mockup

No new pages. Replay runs on the daily page in a "replay" state (the way demo mode does for admins today). What is added is three doors into it, one banner, one result card, one finish screen, one email button and one panel setting. Practice only, nothing stored in votes, no points.

1 · My results — the main door

A missed day inside the window gets a Replay the Day button; it still carries no numbers. A day you played but skipped records on gets Replay the 3 you skipped under its results. A missed day older than the window stays as it is today: listed, closed, no button.

7 · Platform panel · System settings

One field beside the daily schedule. The window is days back from today, by the drop's publish day. 0 turns Replay off for A&Rs. Admins are never capped.

Replay the Day
days
A&Rs can replay the records they did not rate on any published drop from the last this many days. Nothing is stored and no points are awarded. 0 = off. Platform admins can replay any drop.

How you get there, in order of how often it will happen
  1. My results (1): the Replay button on any missed day in the window, and on a played day with skipped records. This is the door most A&Rs will use, and the only one that reaches a day they were never emailed about.
  2. The results email → the daily results screen (2): the panel at the bottom when the day has records they did not rate.
  3. The missed-yesterday email (6): one extra button.
  4. Admins: any published drop, by its My results row or the console's daily screen (a Replay link next to the drop picker; not drawn). The stream reveal and the Track Report pager stay admin-only on the same page.
Not here, on purpose: no note box in replay; no rank line for the bottom of the field; no replay on the front page yet (the anonymous try-it is a separate mockup when you want it); no replay of a drop that has not published.
