3 · Delete — confirm with email code

Delete your account

This is permanent. You'll be signed out and won't be able to log back in to this account.

What we delete

  • Your name, email, phone, photo and social handles
  • Your points, results and place on every board
  • Your Sidebet entry, if the contest is still open

What stays, with no name attached

  • Your ratings, inside past room averages, so the results artists and other A&Rs already received don't change.
  • Your notes to artists, on their reports, signed “A&R”.
  • Your role and city, which are counted in artists' reports.

We sent a code to k•••@gmail.com.

 

You can sign up again later with the same email, but you'll start from zero.
