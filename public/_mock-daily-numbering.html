Two choices before I build it

A. When does a drop get its number? I recommend at open. A drop that is pushed, then moved or deleted before it opens leaves no gap. The cost is that the number isn't known until 6 PM, even though Drupal pushes the track list an hour earlier. The other option, a number at push, shows it sooner, but deleting a pushed drop leaves a gap (for example #46 → #48).
B. A drop that opened and was then deleted: I recommend it keeps its number and the count skips it. Numbers are never reused, because one may already be in a post or an email.
