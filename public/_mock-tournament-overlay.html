Behind it

Mockup with placeholder competitors and songs. The stream is vertical, 1080×1920, for Instagram Live and TikTok LIVE. The overlay is kept clear of the apps' own top bar, comments and buttons (turn on the guides to see them). Scaled to fit. While a poll is listening or voting, the overlay shows only how many A&Rs have voted. The split and the winner appear after the poll is ratified. Nothing here prints a prize.
