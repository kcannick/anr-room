What the numbers mean

Mockup for the A&R Daily rating step (the Live page gets the same). The operator's definitions, word for word. The meaning of the chosen number appears under the dial, and the full list opens from a link. Tap the numbers to try it. Frame D is what the artist sees.

A · Before choosing
The empty box keeps the button from jumping when a number is picked.
B · After choosing 6
One line, the operator's words. Nothing else on the step changes.
C · The full list open
The chosen number is highlighted in the list. "Close" collapses it again.
D · The artist's verdict
The Track Report and the artist email print the meaning of the average's whole number: 6.4 and 6.9 are both 6, Potential Single. It never rounds up into the next verdict, the same way the bands already cut.
