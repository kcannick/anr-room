MOCKUP — resend an artist notice for ONE round. Today artist notices are all-or-nothing per room: the queue is idempotent, so once a round is sent it can never be sent again — if an address had a typo, or the email bounced, there's no way to retry that one artist. This adds a 📨 button to each eligible round in the Rounds tab. It is also the "send just this one" button for a round whose contact you added after the main send.
