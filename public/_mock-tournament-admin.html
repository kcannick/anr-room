Graphics · weekly qualifier series

One set per week as a seat fills: the week's qualifier is the big picture, everyone picked before fills the small seats, the rest are silhouettes. When the eighth seat fills, the final promo puts a question mark in the big slot ("Who has the Best Ear?"). After the event, the champion set. Shown as images, never a download-to-see. The bracket graphic is event-night only and is published from the Wrap card.
